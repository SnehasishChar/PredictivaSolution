The Digital Foundation: Data Generation & Acquisition

You cannot optimize what you cannot measure. The journey to an autonomous, optimized enterprise begins with reliable, high-fidelity data captured directly from the source—your physical assets and operations. At Predictiva AI, we specialize in architecting the secure, robust data pipelines required for sophisticated AI and optimization engines. We transform the chaotic environment of the factory floor into a structured, real-time stream of actionable intelligence.

Data Generation: Sourcing the Truth

We help you identify and deploy the right hardware to capture essential operational parameters. This isn't just about sensors; it is about strategic data mapping to unlock specific business values (like OEE or Predictive Maintenance).

  • Sensors & Automation Systems: Integration with existing PLCs, automation systems, and new sensor deployments (e.g., vibration, temperature, acoustic sensors) to monitor machine health and process variables.
  • Mobile Devices & GPS: Capturing movement data (GPS, acceleration, gyroscopes) for logistics tracking and internal asset management.
  • User Inputs: Structuring the capture of crucial qualitative data, meta-data, and language-based inputs that provide context missing from machine data alone.

Data Analysis and Actionable Insights

Once your data is securely captured and managed, it’s time to unlock its potential. At Predictiva AI, our analytics engine moves beyond descriptive reporting ("What happened?") into the realm of advanced predictive and prescriptive intelligence ("What should we do about it?").

  • Predictive Maintenance: Moving from scheduled downtime to condition-based maintenance, optimizing asset lifespan and minimizing operational disruption.
  • OEE (Overall Equipment Effectiveness): Providing real-time, granular visibility into performance, availability, and quality to drive continuous improvement.
  • Energy Management: Identifying energy drains and prescribing operational changes to optimize consumption and reduce utility costs.

Consulting: A Tailored Approach

Our approach is consultative. We don't just drop off sensors; we work with your team to specify the exact requirements for your desired outcomes.

  • Sensors Selection: Guidance on the most cost-effective and reliable sensors for specific use cases (e.g., condition monitoring vs. simple tracking).
  • Network Architecture: Designing a resilient network foundation that supports high data volumes without disrupting existing operations.
  • Data Acquisition Strategy: A clear roadmap for what data is needed and how it will be used to generate your first set of AI-driven insights.
  • Designing the Performance Management Framework : Identifying the right KPIs and metrics and incorporating predictive and prescriptive capabilities based on metric behavior.
